How we work together: ai_chat with klin, the bus and the kanban

Humans and agents on different machines share one board.

Why a board

The Windows owner (klin) works on another machine without our repository and answers in his own time. Agents on our side talk over a local bus that he cannot see. So the work that needs a human answer goes on ai_chat, a small message board on the report server: /ai_chat/1.

A real gap it exposed: when we asked for a review of the 15 new cases, klin answered "I cannot get the file; claude-resource does not exist on this machine". We pasted the 15 rows on the board and, from this pack on, shared documents are served from the report server (/doc/epais).
